# Problem Intelligence OS



## Mission
To make the world's problem-solving knowledge universally discoverable, connected, and actionable.

## Vision
A future where anyone, anywhere, can instantly discover every meaningful problem, understand every existing solution, identify remaining gaps, and contribute to solving humanity's biggest challenges.


## Why are we building this?
Today, information about innovation is scattered.

Research papers live in one place.

Startups in another.

Patents somewhere else.

Open-source projects elsewhere.

Government initiatives are difficult to discover.

As a result, people unknowingly duplicate work or fail to build on existing knowledge.

Problem Intelligence OS aims to connect these disconnected pieces into one searchable knowledge graph.
